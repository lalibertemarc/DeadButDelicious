# Dead But Delicious

*Every corpse is a meal. Every meal deserves a review.*

A WoW Forever and Classic Era addon for Undead characters. When you Cannibalize a corpse, your character comments on it in `/say` like a food critic, with a line that fits what you're eating:

> Murloc. Salty, with a fishy finish. Mrglrgl to you too.

> Ghost meat. Zero calories. Diet food.

> Kobold. Tastes like candle wax and bad decisions.

## How it works

Target the corpse and Cannibalize with the Dead But Delicious **key binding** (Options > Keybindings > Dead But Delicious), its **icon** (shown next to the target frame while your target is a corpse you can eat, with the cooldown on it), or a macro with `/click DeadButDeliciousButton`. One press casts Cannibalize and says the comment.

The game only lets addons talk during a key press or click, so the comment goes out with the press, just before the cast. Dead But Delicious stays quiet when the cast clearly won't work: you're moving, Cannibalize is on cooldown, or the corpse is more than about 10 yards away.

Cast Cannibalize from your own action bar and you still get a comment, but only you see it (it looks like your own `/say` line in your chat).

On Forever, the game hides what a corpse is in dungeons and raids, so there your critic reviews the place instead of the meal: "Dungeon dining. The ambience is mostly screaming.", "Banquet for forty. I'm taking the leftovers.", and a few lines of its own for each classic dungeon and raid and each of Forever's new dungeons ("Deadmines. Mine-to-table dining.", "Ruins of Lordaeron. Home cooking. I used to live upstairs."). The icon shows while your target is dead; if the game hides even that, it stays hidden, but the key binding still comments out of combat. Classic Era doesn't hide anything, so there you get the corpse's own lines everywhere.

## What it knows

- **Monsters** by name: murlocs, kobolds, gnolls, trolls, ogres, harpies, centaurs, quilboar, naga, furbolgs, troggs, goblins, gnomes, dwarves, orcs, tauren, night elves, the Defias, the Scarlet Crusade, pirates, the Syndicate, cultists, Forsaken, ghosts, skeletons and ghouls, plus a few famous names (Hogger, VanCleef, Herod).
- **Players** by race.
- **Dungeons and raids** on Forever: the place, not the corpse.
- **Anything else** by type (Humanoid or Undead), and a "mystery meat" line when you cast from your bar with no corpse targeted.

Edit every line, and the words that pick them, in `Data.lua`.

## In French, German and Spanish

On a French, German or Spanish (Spain or Mexico) client your critic speaks your language: every line has its own version in `Locales/` (`frFR.lua`, `deDE.lua`, `esES.lua`): "Murloc. Salé, avec une finale iodée.", "Murloc. Salzig, mit fischigem Abgang.", "Múrloc. Salado, con un final a pescado." `/dbd language english` switches back to the English lines, `/dbd language auto` to your client's again. The undead accent only respells English lines, so translated comments are said plain.

Only your own client's language is kept in memory: every other client stops reading those files at their first lines.

## Undead accent

`/dbd accent locked` or `jawless` respells every comment as you say it, a little different each time, in a locked jaw ("Murluc. Chewy. I sweer it gurgled un the wey duwn. Mmf.") or a jawless slur ("Hhish Murloc Forager ish a hhish shtick 'ihh ohhinionsh. Ghhk."). Per character, off by default, and it takes effect right away. With [EzAccent](../EzAccent) loaded, changing your accent there sets this one too (undead is locked jaw, thick undead is jawless, any other accent is none); `/dbd accent` still sets Dead But Delicious' alone. The corpse's name is left alone, and in `/e` mode only the "quoted" comment is accented, not the bite before it. Comments only you see (`/dbd test`, casts from your bar, private mode) get the accent too.

The rules are [EzAccent](../EzAccent)'s: `AccentRules.lua` is generated by its `tools/gen_rules.py --accents undead --out ../DeadButDelicious/AccentRules.lua` from AccentTranslator's `rules.py`, and `Accent.lua` is the undead part of its engine (the same copy as EzMinion's).

## Commands

Use `/dbd` or `/deadbutdelicious` (`/cannibal` and `/foodcritic` work too).

| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| `/dbd say` | Comment in `/say` (default) |
| `/dbd emote` | Comment as an `/e` emote: *Grimbald chews thoughtfully. "Murloc. Salty."* |
| `/dbd private` | Toggle: every comment is for your eyes only |
| `/dbd accent [none\|locked\|jawless]` | Show or set this character's undead accent (locked jaw, jawless), with a sample |
| `/dbd language [auto\|english]` | French, German and Spanish clients: comment in your language (default) or English |
| `/dbd test` | Taste your target (or the last corpse) without eating it, only you see it |
| `/dbd reset` | Reset the icon position (Shift-drag moves it) |

## Install

Install from CurseForge or Wago, or download the zip from the [Releases](../../releases) page and extract the `DeadButDelicious` folder into one of:

```
World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns\
World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\
```
