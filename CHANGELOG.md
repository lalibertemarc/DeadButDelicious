# Dead But Delicious

## v1.3.1

- Listed under **Roleplay** in the game's AddOns list, next to other roleplay addons.

## v1.3.0

- **A table in every dungeon.** On Forever your critic no longer goes quiet in dungeons and raids. The game still hides what a corpse is there, so instead of the meal it reviews the place: "Dungeon dining. The ambience is mostly screaming." In raids: "Banquet for forty. I'm taking the leftovers." Every classic dungeon and raid has a few lines of its own, from the Deadmines ("Mine-to-table dining.") to Naxxramas, and so do Forever's new ones, from the Hall of Thanes to Manor Mistmantle ("Ruins of Lordaeron. Home cooking. I used to live upstairs."). The icon shows while your target is dead, and casts from your bar comment to you as before.
- **A livelier locked jaw.** The undead accent strains in more ways (tss, hmmf, grrk…), doesn't strain at the end of every single line, and sometimes tucks the sound in before the last word: "Huw ere yuu, mmf?" A word shouted in capitals stays in capitals, and words that already have a double s no longer hiss a third one.

## v1.2.0

- **Speak like the dead.** `/dbd accent locked` (or `jawless`) gives this character an undead accent: every comment comes out respelled as you say it, a little different each time. Locked jaw squeezes its vowels through clenched teeth and strains at the end: "Murluc. Chewy. I sweer it gurgled un the wey duwn. Mmf." Jawless slurs and gurgles: "Hhish Murloc Forager ish a hhish shtick 'ihh ohhinionsh. Ghhk." The corpse's name is never respelled, and in `/e` mode only the "quoted" comment is accented, not the bite. `/dbd accent` shows the current one with a sample, `/dbd accent none` turns it off. Takes effect right away, per character. The rules are the same as EzAccent's undead accents, and with EzAccent loaded, changing your accent there sets this one too (undead is locked jaw, undead thick is jawless, anything else is none); `/dbd accent` still changes Dead But Delicious' alone.
- **No more empty boasts.** Clicking the icon or key binding during the global cooldown, or while already casting or eating, no longer says a line without Cannibalize going out, and spam clicks get at most one comment per global cooldown. A press queued in the last moments of the global cooldown, or while bandaging, still gets its line.
- **No more errors on hidden speed.** Forever sometimes hides your speed from addons; the check that skips a comment while you move now handles it quietly.

## v1.1.0

- **Commands that match the name.** `/dbd` and `/deadbutdelicious` now open the addon's commands. `/cannibal` and `/foodcritic` still work.

## v1.0.0

- **Every meal deserves a review.** Cannibalize a corpse with the Dead But Delicious key binding, icon or `/click DeadButDeliciousButton` and your Undead comments on it in `/say`: murloc is salty, ghost meat has zero calories, kobold tastes of candle wax. Lines for 26 kinds of monster, every player race, and a few famous names.
- **Say it your way.** `/cannibal emote` turns comments into `/e` emotes, `/cannibal private` keeps them to yourself, and casts from your own action bar comment to you alone.
- **Know when you're hungry again.** The icon shows Cannibalize's cooldown swipe and countdown, like an action button.
- **Off duty in dungeons and raids** on Forever: the game hides what a corpse is there, so the addon stays quiet rather than review a raptor.
- **Classic Era too.** One download runs on Forever and Classic Era (1.15.9), where it reviews dungeon and raid corpses as well.
