# Dead But Delicious

## v1.1.0

- **Commands that match the name.** `/dbd` and `/deadbutdelicious` now open the addon's commands. `/cannibal` and `/foodcritic` still work.

## v1.0.0

- **Every meal deserves a review.** Cannibalize a corpse with the Dead But Delicious key binding, icon or `/click DeadButDeliciousButton` and your Undead comments on it in `/say`: murloc is salty, ghost meat has zero calories, kobold tastes of candle wax. Lines for 26 kinds of monster, every player race, and a few famous names.
- **Say it your way.** `/cannibal emote` turns comments into `/e` emotes, `/cannibal private` keeps them to yourself, and casts from your own action bar comment to you alone.
- **Know when you're hungry again.** The icon shows Cannibalize's cooldown swipe and countdown, like an action button.
- **Off duty in dungeons and raids** on Forever: the game hides what a corpse is there, so the addon stays quiet rather than review a raptor.
- **Classic Era too.** One download runs on Forever and Classic Era (1.15.9), where it reviews dungeon and raid corpses as well.
